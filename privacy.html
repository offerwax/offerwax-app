Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Offerwax, why it is used and how we aim to protect it.

Last updated: 30 September 2026
Your Privacy Matters

Offerwax should only collect information that is reasonably required to operate accounts, track eligible activity, process rewards, provide support and protect the platform from misuse.

01

Information You Provide

When you create or manage an Offerwax account, information you provide may include:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address, where provided
  • UPI ID or other supported payout details
  • Referral information
  • Information submitted through Support
02

Offer & Tracking Information

When you participate in eligible campaigns, Offerwax may process information needed to determine the status of an offer.

This may include campaign identifiers, referral identifiers and events such as:

  • Offer or referral clicks
  • Install or registration status where available
  • Required-action status
  • Completion and validation status
  • Reward status

The exact tracking information available can depend on the applicable campaign and tracking partner.

03

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate Offerwax and provide its features, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Displaying offer and reward status
  • Processing eligible rewards and withdrawals
  • Operating Refer & Earn
  • Operating Share & Earn
  • Responding to support requests
  • Detecting duplicate, fraudulent or abusive activity
  • Maintaining platform security
04

Payment Information

A UPI ID or another supported payment identifier may be required when you request a withdrawal or when a campaign requires reward payment information.

Payment information should only be used for legitimate reward, withdrawal, verification and support purposes.

Offerwax should never ask you to provide your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, OTP or internet banking password to receive an Offerwax reward.

05

Referral Information

Offerwax may associate a referral code or referral link with an account to determine which eligible account referred another user.

Referral information may be used to calculate applicable referral rewards and prevent self-referral or duplicate-account abuse.

06

Share & Earn

When Share & Earn is used, a campaign link may contain an identifier associated with the user who shared it.

Campaign activity may be associated with that identifier so Offerwax can display relevant clicks, tracked activity, completion status and eligible earnings.

Only information reasonably necessary for the feature should be shown to the person who shared the campaign.

07

Support Requests

When you contact Offerwax Support, the information you submit may be used to identify your account, investigate your request and provide a response.

Support information may include your name, registered mobile number, description of the problem and related account or offer information.

08

Third-Party Services

Offerwax campaigns may direct users to third-party websites, applications, advertisers, tracking providers or other services.

Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Users should review the privacy information provided by the relevant third-party service before submitting personal information to it.

09

Information Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers or campaign partners where reasonably necessary to operate features such as tracking, validation, security, support or payment processing.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Offerwax should not sell users' personal information as a standalone product.

10

Fraud & Security

Information may be processed to identify suspicious activity and protect Offerwax, its users and campaign partners.

Security checks may be used to identify activity such as duplicate accounts, self-referrals, artificial activity or attempts to manipulate campaign tracking.

11

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no internet service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users are also responsible for protecting their account credentials.

12

Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including account operation, reward records, fraud prevention, dispute resolution and applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

13

Your Choices & Requests

Users may contact Offerwax Support regarding questions or requests concerning their account information.

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, users may be able to request correction, access or deletion of certain personal information.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention or legitimate record-keeping purposes.

14

Children's Privacy

Offerwax users must satisfy the age and eligibility requirements applicable to the platform and to each individual offer they choose to participate in.

Certain third-party financial, investment or other services may have their own minimum-age requirements.

15

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Offerwax features, technology, business practices or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be displayed on this page together with its updated date.

🔒 Protect Your Account

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN or banking password with anyone claiming it is required to receive an Offerwax reward.
